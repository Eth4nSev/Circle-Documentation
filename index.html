01 / OVERVIEW

What is Circle?

Circle is a social app built around posts, profiles, smaller private groups called Circles, and direct conversations.

Home Feed Browse posts and filter your feed by the available options.
Posts Create image posts, add captions, and control who can interact with them.
Circles Organize people into smaller groups and share content with specific Circles.
Messaging Send direct messages and use Circle Chat where available.
This site documents the current Circle app rather than future product ideas.

03 / HOME

Home Feed

Home is the main social feed. Posts are displayed directly in the app and the feed can be filtered using the available feed controls.

All View the normal Home feed.
Following Focus the feed on accounts you follow.
Circles Filter the feed to posts from selected Circles.
Refresh Pull down to reload the current feed.

Filter the feed

Open the Home tab.
Tap the feed selector.
Choose the feed or Circle filter you want.
If using Circles, select the Circle you want to view.
04 / POSTS

Posts

Circle posts can contain an image and caption. When creating a post, you can configure its audience and available interaction options.

Create a post

Open the post creation flow.
Select an image from your device.
Add a caption if you want one.
Choose who the post should be shared with.
Configure comments, sharing, and emoji reactions.
Publish the post.

Post interactions

Likes Like posts and view the available like count.
Comments Open a post's comments and participate when comments are enabled.
Emoji Reactions React with emojis when the post allows reactions.
Post Options Access the available actions for a post.

Edit a post

Open the options menu on your own post.
Select Edit.
Change the available post information.
Save the changes.

Delete a post

Open the options menu on your own post.
Select Delete.
Confirm the deletion.
05 / PROFILES

Profiles

Profiles let you view another user's information, posts, and follower relationships.

Open a profile

Tap a username or profile picture from a post.
Circle opens the corresponding profile.
Browse the user's information and posts.

Follow a user

Open the user's profile.
Tap Follow.
The relationship is saved to your account.
Followers View accounts following the profile.
Following View accounts followed by the profile.
06 / CIRCLES

Circles are smaller groups of people that let you organize your social experience and share posts with specific groups.

Create a Circle

Tap the create or plus button.
Enter a Circle name.
Choose the Circle's appearance.
Add people to the Circle.
Create the Circle.

07 / CIRCLE TOOLS

Circle Tools

Each Circle has its own screen with controls for managing and interacting with the group.

Members View the people who belong to the Circle.
Invites Review available Circle invitations.
Settings Access the Circle configuration available to you.
Mute Temporarily mute activity from a Circle.

View Circle members

Circle invites

Open the invites screen.

08 / MESSAGING

Messaging

Circle supports direct conversations and Circle Chat.

Direct messages

Open the messaging flow for another user.
Open the conversation.
Type your message.
Tap send.

Circle Chat

Circle Chat provides a group conversation associated with a Circle when the Circle has chat enabled.

Direct messages and Circle Chat are separate from comments on posts.

11 / PRIVACY

Privacy & Data

Circle uses Supabase for authentication, database data, storage, and realtime functionality.

Authentication Handles authenticated user sessions.
Database Stores profiles, posts, relationships, Circles, comments, and messages.
Storage Stores profile and post media.
Realtime Supports live messaging updates.
Client-side UI checks are not a security boundary. Supabase Row Level Security should enforce access to protected data.
